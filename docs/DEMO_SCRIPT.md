# MYIN Judge Demo — 3 Minutes

Start already logged in. Do not spend judging time on signup.

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem and mission

“Muslim youth opportunities are scattered across WhatsApp, mosque announcements, Instagram, email, flyers, and personal networks. Organizations need youth talent but struggle to find the right students at the right time. MYIN turns that fragmented system into one trusted, intelligent network.”

## 0:30–1:10 — Student value

Open the student home screen.

- Show the best recommended action.
- Open the top match from Today’s Email.
- Show the 100-point breakdown.
- Point to prayer space, Jumu’ah flexibility, compensation, confidence, and missing-data transparency.
- Say: “AI can add context, but this published deterministic rubric—not a hidden model—calculates the ranking.”

## 1:10–1:55 — Organization and Gemini

Switch to `org_test`.

Paste a rough fictional opportunity announcement. Run extraction.

- Show loading state.
- Show editable fields.
- Show extraction confidence and missing safety information.
- Add supervision or deadline if missing.
- Publish.

Say: “Gemini reduces posting friction. It cannot auto-publish, invent missing safety facts, or decide the student match.”

## 1:55–2:30 — Controlled connection

Switch back to `amina_test`.

- Open the newly published opportunity.
- Show the transparent score.
- Click “I’m interested.”
- Open the email draft and explain that nothing is sent.

Switch to the organization.

- Open Candidates.
- Click “View safe profile.”
- Show initials, relevant skills, availability, evidence labels, and no direct contact details.
- Request a controlled introduction.

## 2:30–3:00 — Safety, impact, and scale

Open the Ethics page or point to its link.

“MYIN starts with Muslim youth and community trust. We protect identity before reveal, show AI confidence and uncertainty, and make matching auditable. Long term, this can become infrastructure for Muslim youth development—then extend these same values to schools, nonprofits, and other underserved communities without erasing the original mission.”

Close with:

“Your skills. Your community. Your impact.”
